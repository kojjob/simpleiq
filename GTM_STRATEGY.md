# SimpleIQ Go-to-Market Strategy

## Executive Summary

SimpleIQ's go-to-market strategy focuses on product-led growth combined with targeted outbound efforts to capture the underserved SME analytics market. By offering a freemium model with viral features and focusing on specific high-value verticals, we aim to achieve $20M ARR within 24 months.

---

## Market Entry Strategy

### Positioning Statement

> **For** small and medium-sized businesses who need data insights but lack technical resources,
> **SimpleIQ** is the analytics and AI platform that makes data intelligence as easy as asking a question.
> **Unlike** traditional BI tools that require technical expertise and long implementations,
> **SimpleIQ** offers instant insights through natural language, connecting to your existing tools in minutes, not months.

### Core Messaging Framework

#### Primary Message
*"Turn your business data into answers, not spreadsheets"*

#### Supporting Messages
1. **Simplicity**: "Analytics without the complexity"
2. **Speed**: "From data to decisions in minutes"
3. **Intelligence**: "AI that speaks your language"
4. **Affordability**: "Enterprise insights at SME prices"
5. **Trust**: "Your data, your control, our security"

### Value Proposition Canvas

| Customer Jobs | Pains | Gains |
|--------------|-------|-------|
| Make data-driven decisions | Hours creating reports | 80% time savings |
| Track business performance | Data scattered everywhere | Single source of truth |
| Predict future trends | No predictive capabilities | AI-powered forecasting |
| Share insights with team | Email spreadsheet chaos | Real-time collaboration |
| Prove ROI to stakeholders | Manual calculations | Automated ROI tracking |

## Sales Strategy

### Sales Model Evolution

#### Phase 1: Product-Led Sales (Months 0-6)
- **Focus**: Self-service with product-led insights
- **Team**: 2 customer success reps
- **Process**: Proactive outreach to engaged free users
- **Tools**: Intercom, Calendly, Loom

#### Phase 2: Hybrid Model (Months 7-12)
- **Focus**: Sales-assisted for higher tiers
- **Team**: 2 SDRs + 2 AEs + 1 Sales Manager
- **Process**: Inbound qualification + outbound to target accounts
- **Tools**: HubSpot CRM, Gong, Outreach

#### Phase 3: Enterprise Motion (Year 2)
- **Focus**: Land and expand in mid-market
- **Team**: 5 SDRs + 5 AEs + 2 SEs + 1 Director
- **Process**: Account-based selling
- **Tools**: Salesforce, 6sense, Drift

## Pricing Strategy

### Pricing Philosophy

- **Value-Based**: Price on value delivered, not features
- **Transparent**: No hidden fees or surprise costs
- **Scalable**: Grows with customer success
- **Flexible**: Usage-based components for fairness

### Pricing Tiers

#### Freemium: SimpleIQ Starter ($0/month)
- **Limits**: 1 user, 1 data source, 3 dashboards
- **AI Queries**: 100/month
- **Support**: Community only
- **Purpose**: Product trial and viral growth

#### Professional: SimpleIQ Pro ($49/user/month)
- **Limits**: Up to 10 users, 10 data sources, unlimited dashboards
- **AI Queries**: 5,000/month
- **Support**: Email (24hr response)
- **Features**: All core features
- **Target**: Small teams and growing startups

#### Business: SimpleIQ Business ($149/user/month)
- **Limits**: Unlimited users and data sources
- **AI Queries**: 50,000/month
- **Support**: Priority (2hr response)
- **Features**: + Advanced AI, automation, white-label
- **Target**: Established SMEs

#### Enterprise: SimpleIQ Enterprise (Custom)
- **Starting at**: $1,500/month
- **Includes**: Everything + custom AI models
- **Support**: Dedicated success manager
- **Features**: + SSO, audit logs, SLA
- **Target**: Mid-market companies

### Pricing Tactics

#### Launch Pricing (Months 0-6)
- 50% discount for beta users (lifetime)
- Annual plans: 20% discount
- Non-profit/education: 30% discount

#### Expansion Levers
- Additional AI queries: $20/1000 queries
- Premium connectors: $50/month each
- Custom training: $500/session
- Priority support: $200/month

### Pricing Experiments

- **A/B Testing**: Test price points with different cohorts
- **Feature Gating**: Test which features drive upgrades
- **Usage-Based**: Test consumption pricing model
- **Bundling**: Test vertical-specific packages
