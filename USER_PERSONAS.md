# SimpleIQ User Personas & Use Cases

## Overview

This document defines our target user personas, their specific needs, pain points, and use cases for the SimpleIQ platform. These personas guide our product development, marketing, and customer success strategies.

---

## Primary Personas

### 1. Sarah Chen - The Growing E-commerce Entrepreneur

<img src="persona-sarah.png" alt="Sarah Chen" width="150" style="float:right;margin:20px">

**Demographics**
- Age: 32
- Role: Founder & CEO
- Company: Online fashion boutique
- Employees: 12
- Location: Los Angeles, CA
- Education: BA in Marketing
- Tech Savviness: Medium-High

**Background**
Sarah started her online boutique 3 years ago and has grown it from a side hustle to a $2M/year business. She sells through her website, Amazon, Instagram, and recently opened a pop-up store. She's drowning in data from multiple platforms but lacks the technical skills or budget for a data analyst.

**Goals**
- Understand which products and channels drive the most profit
- Predict inventory needs to avoid stockouts
- Identify and retain high-value customers
- Optimize marketing spend across channels
- Make data-driven decisions quickly

**Pain Points**
- 🔴 Spends 10+ hours/week creating manual reports in Excel
- 🔴 Data scattered across Shopify, Amazon Seller Central, Google Analytics, Instagram
- 🔴 Can't afford $75K/year for a data analyst
- 🔴 Missed $50K in sales last quarter due to stockouts
- 🔴 No real-time visibility into business performance

**Use Cases with SimpleIQ**

#### Use Case 1: Unified Commerce Dashboard
- **Scenario**: Monday morning business review
- **Action**: Opens SimpleIQ, asks "How did we perform last week?"
- **Result**: Sees unified dashboard with sales across all channels, top products, and AI-generated insights
- **Time Saved**: 2 hours vs. manual Excel consolidation

#### Use Case 2: Inventory Prediction
- **Scenario**: Planning next month's inventory order
- **Action**: Asks SimpleIQ "What products will I need to reorder in the next 30 days?"
- **Result**: AI predicts demand based on seasonality, trends, and current stock
- **Value**: Prevents $15K in lost sales from stockouts

#### Use Case 3: Customer Segmentation
- **Scenario**: Planning email marketing campaign
- **Action**: Requests "Show me customers who haven't purchased in 60 days but were high spenders"
- **Result**: Gets segmented list with personalized campaign recommendations
- **Impact**: 25% improvement in email conversion rates

**Quote**
> "I need insights, not more spreadsheets. If a tool can't give me answers in plain English, I don't have time for it."
