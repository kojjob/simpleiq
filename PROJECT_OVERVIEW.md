# SimpleIQ: Modern Analytics & AI Platform for SMEs

## Executive Summary

SimpleIQ is a cloud-native, self-service analytics and AI platform designed to democratize data intelligence for small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs). By eliminating technical barriers and providing an intuitive, conversational interface, SimpleIQ empowers non-technical business users to unlock insights, automate workflows, and make data-driven decisions without requiring specialized data teams or extensive IT resources.

## Risk Assessment & Mitigation

### Technical Risks
- **Risk**: AI model accuracy/hallucinations
- **Mitigation**: Human-in-the-loop validation, confidence scoring

### Market Risks
- **Risk**: Big tech entering SME analytics space
- **Mitigation**: Deep SME focus, rapid innovation, community moat

### Operational Risks
- **Risk**: Scaling customer support
- **Mitigation**: AI-powered support, comprehensive self-service

### Security Risks
- **Risk**: Data breach impacting SME customers
- **Mitigation**: SOC 2 compliance, encryption, regular audits

### Financial Risks
- **Risk**: High customer acquisition costs
- **Mitigation**: Product-led growth, referral programs, partnerships
